Oversight

Every week the watcher algorithm checks two numbers and compares them against thresholds set in advance. The result of every check is published here.

The feed will start filling up together with live trading.
Each entry: date of the check, current drawdown, expectancy over the last 100 trades, status under the rules and the actions taken.

What exactly is checked

Drawdown answers the question “how much does it hurt right now”. Thresholds: up to 27% — normal, 27–34% — attention, 34–41% — review, above 41% — trading halted.

Expectancy over 100 trades answers the question “is the system still working”. The alarm threshold is below −0.05% per trade.

The number of trades is reconciled against the expected one: this is how a stopped or hung algorithm is detected, which would otherwise look simply like a prolonged drawdown.

The watcher makes no trading decisions and never intervenes in trades — it measures and reports. The actions for each level are defined in advance, before things get unpleasant. In detail — in the strategy description.
