How to join

Copy trading is set up once and then runs without your involvement. Below is what you will need, and in what order.

What is required from you

A broker accountHedging is mandatory, not netting. The two algorithms work independently and may hold opposing positions on one instrument; on a netting account the second trade would close the first, and the result would diverge from the strategy.
DepositFrom $1,000 at 1% risk per trade. Below that, some positions will go at the minimum lot with inflated risk. A detailed calculation for other risk levels is in the strategy description.
LeverageFrom 1:100. No more than seven positions are open at once, and half the time no more than two, so there is margin to spare.
TerminalMetaTrader 5. For copying it is enough that the terminal is connected to the network — on your own computer or on a VPS.

The order of steps

A step-by-step guide will appear once the official materials arrive from Tickmill — the request has been sent. Instead of a retelling in my own words, their documents will go here: opening an account, connecting to the signal, setting up the terminal.

What it costs

The fee is taken as a percentage of profit and only from profit: no profit, no fee. The exact terms and the charging mechanism will appear here after the broker replies.

The usual market rate for copy trading is 25–30% of profit. While the strategy has no public record of live trading, the rate is below market. As statistics accumulate it will rise towards the usual one.
